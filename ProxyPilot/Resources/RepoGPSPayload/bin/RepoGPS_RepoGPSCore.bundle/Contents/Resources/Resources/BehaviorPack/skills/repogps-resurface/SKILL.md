# Resurface

Begin with Retrace. Use deterministic history when available to compare the current silence with the repository's own cadence. Re-read durable invariants and current-state sources, inspect changes since the last trustworthy handoff, and label remembered assumptions as confirmed, superseded, or unresolved.

Produce a Season Delta and recommended re-entry sequence. Do not implement repairs during resurfacing.
